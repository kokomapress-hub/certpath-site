FAQ

PMP Practice — Common Questions

Which PMP exam are these tests for?

They are built to the NEW July 2026 PMP Examination Content Outline (People 33% / Process 41% / Business Environment 26%) and the PMBOK® Guide, Eighth Edition — covering predictive, agile, and hybrid ways of working.

How many questions and tests are included?

Four full-length practice exams of 180 questions each — 720 questions total — each mirroring the real 180-question, 240-minute format, with detailed answer explanations.

Why can't I open Test 2 right away?

The tests unlock in order. Finish Practice Test 1 and Test 2 opens; finish Test 2 and Test 3 opens, and so on. This builds your pacing and stamina the same way the real exam journey does.

Do the tests include the new 2026 question formats?

Yes. Alongside single-answer items, the exams include select-two (multiple-response) questions, graphic/exhibit questions (EVM charts, network diagrams, burndown/burnup, control charts, dashboards) and linked case-sets — the more interactive, scenario-based style of the 2026 exam.

How do I get access?

Enter the access code printed on the last page of your CertPath PMP book at certpathpublishing.store/access. Your tests are timed, auto-scored, and work on any device.
