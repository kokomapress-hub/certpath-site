Before you apply

Eligibility, fees & test day

Eligibility

Meet one of these, plus 35 hours of PM education (or a current CAPM):

  • Four-year degree + 36 months leading projects
  • High-school / associate degree + 60 months leading projects

Fees & registration

PMI member$425
Non-member$675

Apply on the PMI website with proof of eligibility. PMI adjusts fees periodically — confirm current pricing on pmi.org.

Test day

Take it at a Pearson VUE center or online with a proctor. Arrive early, present photo ID, sign the NDA. Two 10-minute breaks.

Scoring & attempts

Criterion-based scoring; no published pass mark. Preliminary result at the center, official report in a few days. Up to three attempts within your one-year eligibility period.
