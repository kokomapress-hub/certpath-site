CNOR — Common Questions

How many questions are on the CNOR exam?

The CNOR exam has 200 multiple-choice questions (about 185 scored plus unscored pretest items) with a 3 hour 45 minute time limit, covering the CCI perioperative nursing blueprint.

What subject areas does the CNOR cover?

CCI organizes the exam around perioperative practice — preoperative assessment and plan of care, intraoperative care, communication and documentation, transfer of care, instrument processing and supply management, emergency situations, and professional practice. Our online bank is organized by the same subject areas.

Do I meet the requirements to sit for the CNOR?

CCI requires a current unrestricted RN (or equivalent) license and a minimum of two years and 2,400 hours of experience in perioperative nursing, with at least 50% (1,200 hours) in the intraoperative setting. Check cc-institute.org for current eligibility.

What is included with the CertPath CNOR book?
