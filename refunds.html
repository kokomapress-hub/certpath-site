Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Digital products (courses, practice packs)

If something isn't working or the product isn't what you expected, email support@certpathpublishing.store with your order email. We will fix your access first and, if we can't make it right, work with you on a refund.

Books bought on Amazon

Paperback, hardcover and Kindle editions are sold by Amazon, so returns and refunds follow Amazon's policies. The online practice tests that come with a book stay available through your printed access code.

Access problems

Most issues — a code that won't unlock, or tests missing on a new device — can be fixed quickly. Email us from the address you used to buy, and include your access code or license key.
