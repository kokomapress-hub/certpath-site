CAPM exam outline — 2023

What's actually on the CAPM exam

150 questions in about 3 hours (180 minutes) — across the four current CAPM domains:

Fundamentals & Core Concepts

36%
  • Projects, programs & portfolios
  • Life cycles & development approaches
  • Roles, ethics & stakeholders
  • Planning basics & artifacts
  • Issues, risks, assumptions & constraints

Predictive Methodologies

17%
  • Process groups & components
  • WBS & work packages
  • Critical path & scheduling
  • Cost/schedule variance (EVM)
  • Integration & change control

Agile Frameworks

20%
  • Adaptive mindset & when it fits
  • Iterative vs incremental
  • Scrum, Kanban, XP & SAFe
  • Backlogs & adaptive artifacts
  • Adaptive tracking

Business Analysis

27%
  • Elicitation techniques
  • Requirement types & documentation
  • Traceability matrix & backlog
  • Prioritization (MoSCoW)
  • Roadmaps & validation
Before you apply

Eligibility, fees & test day

Eligibility

No project experience required. You need:

  • A secondary degree (high-school diploma, GED or global equivalent)
  • 23 hours of project management education before the exam

Fees & registration

PMI member$225
Non-member$300

Fees as published by PMI; confirm current pricing on pmi.org when you apply.

Test day

Take it at a Pearson VUE center or online with a proctor. Arrive early, present photo ID, sign the NDA. 150 questions in 3 hours, with one optional 10-minute break.

Scoring & attempts

Criterion-based scoring; no published pass mark. Preliminary result at the center, official report in a few days. Up to three attempts within your one-year eligibility period.
