Understand the idea first.

PMP Exam Prep · Chapter 3, page 26Figure 3.1
Free PMP practice exam · Question 1 of 180

A newly formed hybrid team drawn from three departments describes the project's purpose differently in casual conversation. What should the project manager do FIRST?

  1. ADraft a vision statement from the charter and circulate it to the leads for comment.
  2. BHave the sponsor issue a formal statement of purpose to settle the differences.
  3. CFacilitate a session with the sponsor and department leads to co-create a shared vision.
  4. DDecompose the requirements in detail so the differing interpretations converge.
Answer explanation
  1. CFacilitate a session with the sponsor and department leads to co-create a shared vision.

A shared vision is built collaboratively with the sponsor and key leads. Drafting from the charter and circulating for comment feels efficient but imposes one interpretation; a sponsor edict settles by authority without buy-in.

Closest distractor: A — a draft to react to is not the same as co-creating shared understanding.
