Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

CertPath Publishing ("we") publishes exam-preparation books and runs this website. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you enter it, for example to start a free practice exam or join our study-tips list. We use MailerLite to store it and send email.
  • Purchase details — digital products are sold through Payhip, which processes your payment. We never see or store your card details. We receive your name, email address and license key so we can give you access.
  • Access codes and license keys — when you unlock a book or course, we check the code with our server (and, for course keys, with Payhip).
  • Progress in your browser — which tests you have unlocked, your scores and lesson progress are saved in your own browser (local storage). They stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Technical data — the site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

To give you the practice tests and courses you asked for, to send the emails you signed up for, to answer support requests, and to keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing support@certpathpublishing.store.
  • You can clear your saved progress at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Children

Our free tools are intended for adult learners and students preparing for exams; we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
