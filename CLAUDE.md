# CertPath blog publishing — standing rules

These apply on top of the scheduled blog-publishing prompt, added per the site owner's request:

- Every blog article must **feature the specific CertPath book tied to the exam** it covers — name it, and for TYPE A articles mention it near the top as the source of the practice-question style.
- Every blog article must include **at least one link to that book's Amazon listing** (`amazonUrl` in `data/books.json`) so readers can buy it, in addition to the existing `/sample?book=<slug>` free-practice link and the exam page link. Do not state the book's price (still applies).
- A good pattern: one Amazon mention in the intro, one small "Featured book" callout box with the cover image (`cover` field in `data/books.json`) and an "Buy on Amazon →" button, and one more Amazon link near the closing paragraph alongside the exam-page link.
